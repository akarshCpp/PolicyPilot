# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees, including interns who have completed their first 30 days, are eligible to work from home. 

## Allowance
Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. The specific days must be agreed upon with the direct manager.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop for all employees. Additional home office equipment (monitor, chair) can be reimbursed up to $500 per year.
