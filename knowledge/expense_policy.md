# Expense Policy

## Meal Allowance
Employees traveling on business are entitled to a daily meal per diem of $75. Receipts are not required for the per diem.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses are capped at $150 per person. Detailed receipts and the list of attendees must be submitted.

## Submission
All expenses must be submitted via the expense portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
