# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance
The company covers 100% of the premium for the standard employee health insurance plan, and 80% for dependents.

## Retirement Plan
The company offers a 401(k) plan with a 100% match up to 4% of the employee's base salary.

## Wellness Stipend
Employees receive a monthly stipend of $100 that can be used for gym memberships, wellness apps, or massage therapy.
