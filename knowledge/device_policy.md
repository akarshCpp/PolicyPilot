# Device Policy

## Acceptable Use
Company-issued laptops and phones are for business use primarily. Reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not violate security policies.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
In the event a company device is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the IT helpdesk immediately so the device can be remotely wiped.

## Device Replacement
Laptops are eligible for replacement every 3 years. Mobile phones are eligible every 2 years.
