# Attendance Policy

## Core Working Hours
The core working hours for all employees are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday through Friday. All employees are expected to be available during these hours.

## Punctuality
Employees are expected to log in on time. Repeated tardiness without notice may result in disciplinary action.

## Flexible Working
Employees may adjust their start and end times around the core working hours, provided they complete a 40-hour workweek.
