# Remote Work Policy

## Definition
Remote work refers to employees whose primary work location is outside a company office (e.g., in a different state or country). This differs from the Work From Home policy which applies to office-based employees.

## Core Hours Alignment
Remote workers must align their schedules to overlap with at least 4 hours of the company's core hours (10 AM to 4 PM EST).

## Tax and Compliance
Remote workers must inform HR immediately if they change their state or country of residence to ensure tax compliance.
