# Leave Policy

## Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 casual leave days per calendar year. Casual leave must be requested at least 2 days in advance unless it is an emergency. Casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 sick leave days per year. Unused sick leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Annual Leave
Employees accrue 15 days of annual leave per year. Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.
